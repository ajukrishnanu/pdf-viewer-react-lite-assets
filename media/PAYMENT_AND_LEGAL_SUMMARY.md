### Indian Law Penalties:

#### Copyright Act, 1957:
- **Section 63**: Imprisonment 6 months to 3 years + Fine ₹50,000 to ₹2,00,000
- **Section 63A**: Enhanced penalties for repeat offenses
- **Section 63B**: Using infringing software - Imprisonment up to 7 years + Fine up to ₹2,00,000
- **Section 64**: Police can seize infringing copies
- **Section 65**: Possession of tools for infringement

#### Information Technology Act, 2000:
- **Section 43**: Compensation up to ₹1,00,00,000
- **Section 66**: Computer offenses - Imprisonment up to 3 years + Fine up to ₹5,00,000
- **Section 66B**: Receiving stolen software - Imprisonment up to 3 years + Fine up to ₹1,00,000
- **Section 66C**: Identity theft - Imprisonment up to 3 years + Fine up to ₹1,00,000
- **Section 66D**: Cheating by personation - Imprisonment up to 3 years + Fine up to ₹1,00,000

#### Indian Penal Code, 1860:
- **Section 420**: Cheating
- **Section 425**: Mischief
- **Section 426**: Punishment for mischief
